# Ramen v15 sound sources

Sound design for `viz/explore/ramen-meisho-v15-sound.html`. Built by `viz/tools/build-ramen-v15-audio.py`; cue times are in `mix.json`.
None of this is a recording made at a ramen shop, in Yokohama, Asakusa, Sapporo or Niigata.

## Synthesized in the build script

Room tone (brown noise, no voices), chopstick/bowl clinks, slurps, ship horn, noodle slaps, geta steps, speech-bubble pops, bowl set-downs, the low tone under the slur, brush strokes, seal stamps, page turns.

## Music (master.m4a only)

Synthesized in the build script: plucked-string tones and a soft pad in a D yo-scale (D E G A B), about 66 BPM, no drums, no vocals, ducked under key cues. It is a reference score, not a recording of koto or shamisen and not an existing song. `effects.m4a` is the same mix without music.
